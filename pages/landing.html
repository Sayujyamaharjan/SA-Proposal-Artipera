THE VERIFICATION SYSTEM

Knowing who you're hiring
starts right here.

It’s a four-step filter to ensure you get a real, reliable professional.

CHECKPOINT 1

Identity verification

We check official government IDs and documents to make sure everyone is exactly who they say they are. No fake names, no fake profiles.

Required for every professional
CHECKPOINT 2

Skill verfication

We review your licenses and past work experience to match you with the right job category.

Required for every professional
CHECKPOINT 3

Manual admin approval

Our team personally reviews and approves every profile to ensure quality.

Required for every professional
CHECKPOINT 4

Review & rating system

Every booking ends in a verified review. Reputation is earned job by job and visible on every profile.

Required for every professional

Our Mission

Connecting people with

Finding the right professional in Nepal can be frustrating when you're stuck relying on casual recommendations. You deserve a clearer, faster way to find quality help. Our platform brings accountability to local services. Customers get a secure space to find, review, and book skilled workers based on real performance. Meanwhile, local professionals gain a powerful platform to market their skills and build their reputations. Together, we are building a more organized and trustworthy service ecosystem for Nepal.
